Anthropic AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations

Introduction to AI Fluency

AI Fluency is the ability to collaborate effectively with AI systems.

The 4D Framework

Delegation

Deciding what AI should do.

Description

Giving AI clear instructions.

Discernment

Evaluating AI responses.

Diligence

Checking AI results.

Generative AI Fundamentals

Generative AI creates new content based on patterns learned from data.

  • Text Generation
  • Code Generation
  • Image Creation
  • Summarization
  • Problem Solving

Capabilities & Limitations

AI can increase productivity but requires human judgement, verification, and responsible usage.

Effective Prompting Techniques

Good prompts provide context, instructions, examples, and expected outcomes.
