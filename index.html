Anthropic AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations

Introduction to AI Fluency

AI Fluency is the ability to effectively collaborate with AI systems by understanding their capabilities, limitations, and best practices.

The AI Fluency Framework

The framework helps humans build effective working relationships with AI through structured interaction and continuous improvement.

The 4D Framework

1. Delegation

Understanding which tasks should be handled by AI and which require human decision making.

2. Description

Providing clear instructions, context, goals, and constraints to get better AI results.

3. Discernment

Critically evaluating AI outputs for accuracy, usefulness, and quality.

4. Diligence

Verifying information, testing results, and ensuring responsible AI usage.
